San Diego isn't Green Bay. The weather is better, the ocean is right there, and you're probably wearing flip-flops. But that doesn't mean you can't find a real home for Packers football — and in Mission Beach, Miss B's Coconut Club has become exactly that.

Every Green Bay Packers game, regular season through playoffs, plays on the outdoor screens at 3704 Mission Blvd. If you're looking for the best place to watch the Packers in San Diego, this is it.

## Why Mission Beach Is a Great Place to Watch Football

There's something fitting about watching the Packers from a coastal open-air bar. The whole point is being somewhere you actually want to be — not a dark basement sports bar, but a place with fresh air, good drinks, and people who are actually into the game.

Mission Beach sits steps from the Pacific on Mission Blvd, and Miss B's takes full advantage of the setting. The outdoor patio and open-air bar make game day feel like a celebration whether the Packers win or lose (mostly win).

## What Miss B's Brings to Game Day

Miss B's isn't a generic sports bar — it's a coastal restaurant and bar that happens to be serious about Packers watch parties. Here's what you'll find on game day:

- **Outdoor screens** — games play across the open-air bar and patio
- **Game day drink specials** — rotating deals on beers, cocktails, and shots
- **Full kitchen** — brunch, lunch, and dinner service all day (kitchen runs until close)
- **Open-air atmosphere** — no stuffy interior, just Mission Beach sun and good company
- **Walk-ins welcome** — show up and find your spot, or [reserve a table](/contact) for busy matchups

The bar opens daily at 11am Monday through Friday, 10am Saturday, and 9:30am Sunday — which means early kickoffs are covered. East Coast noon games hit at 9am PT, and Miss B's opens in time for every one of them on Sundays.

## The Packers Community at Miss B's

One of the things that makes game day at Miss B's work is the crowd. Packers fans in San Diego found each other here, and the energy on game day — especially for big divisional matchups or playoff runs — is the kind you'd expect at a dedicated fan bar.

If you've been watching Packers games at home or bouncing between random bars, come see what a consistent watch party spot does for the experience. It's different when the whole room is rooting the same way.

## What to Order

The [menu at Miss B's](/menu) was built for long afternoons — exactly what game day calls for. A few game day favorites:

- **Coconut Club Burger** — the go-to, no questions
- **Miss B's Favorites Tacos** — shareable, ideal for the first quarter
- **Poke Bowl** — lighter option if you're pacing yourself through a long game
- **Caribbean BBQ Chicken Salad** — solid for the whole-game crowd

On the drinks side, Miss B's runs a full tropical cocktail program alongside draft beers and classic game day orders. Game day specials change week to week — ask your server what's running.

And if it's a morning kickoff, [brunch](/brunch) runs daily until 2pm. Mimosas and the Packers is a perfectly reasonable choice.
