San Diego's college-football crowd includes local fans and transplants supporting programs from around the country. San Diego State has its own football program, while visiting alumni and fans also gather to follow their teams on Saturdays.

Miss B's Coconut Club in Mission Beach has become a place for college football Saturdays. Outdoor screens, the open-air coastal setting, food during kitchen hours, and a crowd that treats college football with the attention it deserves.

## College Football Fans in San Diego

San Diego draws transplants from all over — Oregon, the Midwest, the Southeast, the Pacific Northwest. Most of them grew up watching college football and followed their schools into adulthood. Once they're here, finding a consistent college football home base takes some effort.

Miss B's works because it's not trying to be everything to everyone. The outdoor patio and open-air bar at 3704 Mission Blvd is a genuinely good place to spend a Saturday afternoon, and the commitment to showing games — not just NFL — makes it a natural fit for college football fans who want more than a sports bar interior with bad sightlines.

## What Miss B's Offers on College Football Saturdays

The setup at Miss B's is built for a full day of college football:

- **Outdoor screens** across the open-air bar and patio — multiple games visible
- **Saturday viewing** — check current opening hours before planning around a morning kickoff
- **Game day drink specials** rotating weekly — ask your server what's running
- **[Brunch](/brunch) until 2pm** — breakfast plates, mimosas, and morning cocktails through the early games
- **Kitchen service until 9pm** — explore the [menu](/menu) for food during service

College football kickoff times vary. Check the [current Ducks schedule](/sports) and Miss B's [posted opening hours](/contact) before planning around a particular game.

## Oregon Ducks as the House Team

Miss B's has established the [Oregon Ducks](/blog/watch-oregon-ducks-mission-beach) as the house college football team. Every Ducks game shows on the outdoor screens with the full game day treatment — specials, crowd, the works.

Oregon's Big Ten schedule brings high-profile matchups and fans to watch together at Miss B's. See the [current Ducks dates, opponents and kickoff times](/sports) before planning a game day.

If you're an Oregon fan, this is your San Diego home base. If you're a fan of another program, you're still in good company — Miss B's shows other major games across the schedule, and the atmosphere on a college football Saturday is the same regardless of who you're rooting for.

## Why a Coastal Bar Beats a Traditional Sports Bar for College Football

The standard college football sports bar — neon carpet, forty TVs at awkward angles, no windows — works fine. But it's not the only option, and for a six-hour Saturday session it's not the best one.

Miss B's is an open-air coastal bar steps from the Pacific. College football at noon PT with San Diego sun, a cold drink, and good food is a genuinely different experience than a dark interior. The energy is relaxed until the game gets good, and then it gets loud the same way any watch party does — just with better air circulation.

For fans who grew up watching fall football in real autumn weather, this is the West Coast version. The setting is different, but the commitment to the game is the same.

## What to Eat and Drink

The [Miss B's menu](/menu) offers coastal-inspired food during kitchen hours, which end at 9pm. For college football Saturdays:

- **Coconut Club Burger** — the standard game day order, built to last
- **Miss B's Favorites Tacos** — easy to share, works well through the first half
- **Caribbean BBQ Chicken Salad** — solid if you're pacing yourself through a long day of games
- **Poke Bowl** — lighter option for a multiple-game afternoon

For early kickoffs, [brunch](/brunch) runs until 2pm with full breakfast plates alongside the regular menu. A 10am Big Ten game with brunch items and a mimosa is a legitimate college football Saturday.

On drinks, Miss B's runs a full tropical cocktail program alongside draft beer and classic game day orders. Game day specials change week to week.

**Arrive early for marquee games.** Oregon vs. Ohio State, late-season games with playoff implications, rivalry week — these fill up. Give yourself 20–30 minutes before kickoff to find a good spot.

**Reserve for games that matter.** Walk-ins are always welcome for regular Saturdays. For the big ones, a reservation locks in your table. [Book here](/contact).

**Check the schedule.** The full Oregon Ducks schedule with Pacific kickoff times is on the [Miss B's sports page](/sports). Big Ten games are mostly Saturday, with occasional Friday and weeknight exceptions.

**Check the next game.** If you're staying after your team's game, ask which matchups are on and check the [posted venue hours](/contact). Order food before the kitchen closes at 9pm.
