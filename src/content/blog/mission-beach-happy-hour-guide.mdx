Miss B's Coconut Club is a Mission Beach restaurant and bar at **3704 Mission Blvd**. Its current site-listed happy hour is **Monday through Friday, 3pm to 5pm**; no weekend happy hour is listed. It is a chance to settle in for drinks and food near the beach, whether you're meeting one friend or bringing a group. Confirm the day's offers with Miss B's if a particular special is central to your plans.

## When and Where to Go

The weekday window makes happy hour an option after beach time or before dinner. Miss B's is on Mission Blvd, a short walk from the beach. The [contact page](/contact) has the address, directions, current posted hours and reservation link. Use it before setting out, especially if you're planning around a specific arrival time.

## Find the Right Seat

The large wraparound patio has fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables, weather permitting. Indoors, guests can choose the full bar, booths or tables. That mix gives a small group options for a casual drink and gives diners a place to sit for a meal. Browse [photos and details of the space](/space) to see which setting suits your visit; ask about availability when you arrive or reserve if a table matters.

## Plan Food and Drinks Together

The regular [food and drink menu](/menu) gives you a starting point for Caribbean-inspired dishes, burgers, tacos, tropical cocktails and beer. Happy-hour selections are separate from the regular menu and may change. The site does not publish a dependable item-by-item list of current happy-hour prices, so ask the bar what's included and what each item costs before ordering. Kitchen service is available until **9pm** if your visit continues beyond happy hour.

## Coming With a Group?

Walk-ins are welcome at the bar. For a group that needs a table at a set time, use the [reservation details](/contact). If you need reserved space or a planned celebration, see [private-event options](/private-events) and ask the team what is available for your date. The [space page](/space) helps you describe the indoor or patio setting you have in mind.

For a quick visit, check the posted hours, look over the regular menu, and ask the team about today's happy-hour offers. That gives you accurate details without relying on an old specials list.
