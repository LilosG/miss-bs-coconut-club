Happy hour at Miss B's Coconut Club brings tropical drinks and food to 3704 Mission Blvd in Mission Beach. The current site schedule lists **Monday through Friday, 3pm to 5pm**, with no weekend happy hour. Confirm today's hours and specials if your visit depends on a particular offer.

## Choose a Seat

The wraparound patio has fire pits, outdoor tables and bar seating. Inside, you'll find the full bar, booths and tables. See [photos of the space](/space) to get a feel for the setting before you arrive.

## Choose Food and Drinks

Browse the [food and drink menu](/menu) for Caribbean-inspired dishes, cocktails, beer and other options. Ask the team which food and drinks are included in the current happy hour. Walk-ins are welcome; for a group, check [reservations and directions](/contact).
