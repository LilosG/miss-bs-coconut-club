If you're estimating the cost of a Mission Beach happy hour, start with what is actually published. Miss B's Coconut Club at **3704 Mission Blvd** currently lists happy hour **Monday through Friday, 3pm to 5pm**. It does **not** publish a reliable, item-by-item list of current happy-hour specials and prices on this site. Ask the bar for today's selection and prices before ordering; no specific discount or total can be promised from an older guide.

## What Can You Check in Advance?

The [regular food and drink menu](/menu) shows the kind of choices available, including Caribbean-inspired food, burgers, tacos, tropical cocktails and beer. It is useful for deciding whether the broader menu suits your group. A regular-menu item is not necessarily a happy-hour special. The team can tell you which food or drinks, if any, have a current offer and what those items cost.

The [contact page](/contact) provides posted venue hours, directions and a way to ask questions before visiting. If a particular special determines your budget, contact Miss B's directly rather than assuming a menu price or discount applies during happy hour.

## Plan for Food as Well as Drinks

Decide whether you want a quick drink or a full meal. The regular menu gives you food options even if today's happy-hour selection is limited. Kitchen service is available until **9pm**, so you can ask about a meal if you stay after the listed happy-hour window. A sensible budget accounts for the specific items your party orders; this page cannot supply a trustworthy per-person figure without current prices and choices.

## Does Seating Affect the Plan?

Miss B's has indoor bar, booth and table seating, plus a large wraparound patio with fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables, weather permitting. View [the space](/space) if patio seating is part of your visit. Seating availability and any event arrangement should be confirmed with the team rather than treated as part of a listed drink price.

## What About a Group or Private Event?

Walk-ins are welcome at the bar. If you need a table at a set time, use the [reservation information](/contact). For a larger gathering, the [private-events page](/private-events) explains the available event options. Ask the team for pricing based on your date, group size, space and menu choices; ordinary happy-hour assumptions do not establish a private-event quote.

For current happy-hour details in one place, see the [Miss B's happy-hour guide](/blog/mission-beach-happy-hour-guide). The reliable planning sequence is to check the posted window, browse the regular menu, then confirm today's offers and prices with the venue.
