Miss B's Coconut Club brings tropical cocktails, Caribbean-inspired food and a large wraparound patio to 3704 Mission Blvd in Mission Beach. If you're planning around happy hour, the site's current schedule lists **Monday through Friday, 3pm to 5pm**. Confirm the current selection and prices with the bar before you visit.

## Choosing Food and Drinks

Start with the [food and drink menu](/menu) to see the regular lineup of cocktails, beer and plates. The bar can tell you which items are included in the current happy hour. The patio has fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables; indoor booths and tables are available too.

## Planning a Group Visit

For a casual visit, walk-ins are welcome. If you're coming with a group, check [hours and reservations](/contact) or ask about [private events](/private-events). Pricing for a private gathering depends on the date, space and menu, so the events team can provide details for your plans.
