A beach day at Mission Beach doesn't end when the sun starts dropping. The real move is timing your ocean time with happy hour at Miss B's Coconut Club, where the fire pit patio glows as the sky turns golden and the walk-up counter keeps the tropical cocktails flowing. Here's how to orchestrate a full day that keeps you in the water, on the sand, and at the tiki bar—without wasting time on logistics.

## Step 1: Arrive Early and Claim Your Beach Real Estate

Hit Mission Beach by 9:30 AM. This is non-negotiable if you want parking and a decent stretch of sand. The beach fills fast on weekends, especially in summer, and you'll spend your whole morning circling for a spot if you arrive after 11 AM.

Park along Mission Blvd or use one of the nearby paid lots (they're usually $5–$10 for the day). Grab a coffee and breakfast before you settle on the sand—you'll want fuel before a full morning of swimming and lounging. The beach here faces west, so morning sun is softer and better for swimming without the intense afternoon heat beating down on you.

Bring sunscreen, a hat, and a lightweight cover-up. You're going to be walking from the beach directly to a bar later, and you'll want something to throw on that's easy to move in.

## Step 2: Spend Your Mid-Day at the Water and Boardwalk

Mission Beach offers consistent water conditions—no sharp rocks, a gentle slope into the water, and lifeguards on duty. Swim during the morning and early afternoon when the water is calmest. The beach itself is wide, so even on busy days you can find a quiet spot if you walk north or south a few blocks.

If you need a break from the water, the Mission Beach boardwalk is steps away. Walk the path, grab lunch at one of the casual spots nearby, or just sit on the sand and watch the water. You're building appetite and thirst for what's coming at 4 PM.

By 2 PM, you'll likely want to get out of direct sun. This is the perfect time to rinse off at a beach shower (there are public ones along the boardwalk) and start thinking about the transition to happy hour.

## Step 3: Transition to Miss B's Before Happy Hour Hits

Miss B's happy hour starts at 4 PM. Aim to arrive by 3:45 PM—early enough to grab a good spot before the crowd, late enough that you're not sitting alone. The fire pit patio is the heart of the place, and it fills up fast once word spreads that happy hour is live.

You don't need to go far. Miss B's is at 3704 Mission Blvd, directly on the beach boulevard. Walk from the sand in your cover-up and flip-flops. Grab a beach shower if you want, but honestly, the casual vibe here means nobody's expecting you to look polished. You're coming from the beach—that's the whole point.

## Step 4: Hit the Walk-Up Counter for Speed and Value

Miss B's walk-up counter is designed for this exact moment: you're thirsty, you want a strong cocktail, and you don't want to wait for table service. This is where you order drinks fast and move to the fire pit patio or grab a spot at the tiki bar.

Order a Flamingo Punch, Mai Tai, or Piña Colada—these are the signature drinks, and they hit different when you're still warm from the beach. The happy hour pricing makes these cocktails a steal compared to full-price menu rates. If you're hungry, grab an appetizer from the counter menu: the coconut shrimp or fish tacos pair perfectly with the tropical drinks.

The beauty of the walk-up counter is you're not locked into a table. Grab your drink, walk around, see the space, and decide if you want to settle in or keep it quick.

## Step 5: Settle Into the Fire Pit Patio for the Evening

If you're staying for dinner or just want to ride out happy hour, the fire pit patio is where the energy is. This is the reason to stay past one drink. The fire pits create warmth as the sun drops, the tiki torches light up, and the crowd shifts from beach-day energy to evening social mode.

Grab a table or a spot at the bar overlooking the patio. Order another round from happy hour, or move to full-price cocktails if you want something different. The menu here is built for sharing—order the fish tacos, the coconut shrimp, or the grilled fish plate and settle in.

The fire pit patio is also the spot for people-watching. You'll see the mix of locals, tourists, families transitioning to dinner, and groups celebrating. It's a social space by design, so don't be surprised if you end up chatting with neighbors at the next table.

## Step 6: Decide Your End Game

By 6 or 7 PM, you have options. Stay for dinner and make this a full beach-to-bar day. Leave after happy hour and head back to your hotel or home. Or stay into the evening and let the fire pit patio become your dinner spot and social hub for the night.

If you're planning to stay for dinner, the kitchen here takes the food seriously—this isn't just a drinking bar. The fresh fish, the tropical sides, and the cocktail program all work together. You're not making a compromise; you're choosing to extend a good day.

If you're leaving after happy hour, you've had a full beach day, a strong happy hour experience, and you're back to your car or hotel by 7 PM. That's a clean, efficient day.

## Timing Tips and Logistics

**Parking:** Arrive before 10 AM for easy beach parking. If you're only coming for happy hour, parking is easier after 4 PM when beach visitors are leaving.

**Sunburn Prevention:** Apply sunscreen at 11 AM, 1 PM, and again before you leave the beach. The reflection off the water and sand intensifies UV exposure.

**Cash and Cards:** Miss B's takes both, but the walk-up counter moves faster if you know how you're paying. Have your card or cash ready.

**Restrooms and Showers:** Use the public beach showers along the boardwalk before you head to Miss B's. The bar has restrooms, but they're for customers.

**Water and Hydration:** Drink water between cocktails. You've been in the sun all day, and alcohol dehydrates you faster in heat. Miss B's will fill a water cup if you ask.

## The Real Advantage of This Plan

The genius of this beach day plan is that you're not choosing between beach time and bar time—you're stacking them. You get a full morning and afternoon in the water and sand, then transition directly to a place where the whole point is tropical drinks, fire pit warmth, and social energy. No car drives, no outfit changes, no wasted time. You walk from the sand to the bar, order a cocktail at the walk-up counter, and settle into the fire pit patio as the sun drops.

This is how Mission Beach works when you plan it right. Start early, swim hard, time your happy hour arrival, and let the evening unfold at Miss B's.
