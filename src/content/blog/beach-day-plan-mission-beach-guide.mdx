A Mission Beach day can include beach time and a meal without leaving the neighborhood. Miss B's Coconut Club is at **3704 Mission Blvd**, a short walk from the Pacific. It offers indoor seating, a wraparound patio, food and cocktails, so you can make it a stop before or after time near the sand.

## Choose When to Eat

If a morning meal is part of the plan, Miss B's serves [brunch](/brunch) daily until **2pm**. The brunch menu includes breakfast favorites, mimosas and morning cocktails. Check the [contact page](/contact) for the current posted opening time before choosing an arrival time; the brunch end time alone does not tell you when service begins.

For a later stop, browse the regular [food and drink menu](/menu). Caribbean-inspired dishes, burgers, tacos, tropical cocktails and beer give a group more than one way to round out the day. Kitchen service is available until **9pm**. If you are planning around weekday happy hour, see the [current site-listed schedule and what to confirm](/blog/mission-beach-happy-hour-guide) before relying on a particular offer.

## Pick an Indoor or Outdoor Seat

The [large wraparound patio](/space) has fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables, weather permitting. Indoors are a full bar, booths and tables. The space page has photos, which can help a group decide whether it would prefer a patio table or indoor seating after beach time. Ask the team about availability for the visit you have in mind.

## Keep the Day Flexible

You can eat before heading toward the beach, stop in after a walk, or make Miss B's the place to meet friends. The beach and boardwalk are nearby, but beach conditions and facilities change; check relevant local information separately if those details matter to your outing. This guide focuses on the food, drinks and seating Miss B's provides.

## Find the Restaurant and Plan a Table

Use the [directions and reservations page](/contact) for the address, map, phone and reservation link. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar. If your group needs a particular table time, make a reservation; for a larger planned gathering, see [private-event options](/private-events). A little coordination around the meal leaves the rest of your Mission Beach day open.
