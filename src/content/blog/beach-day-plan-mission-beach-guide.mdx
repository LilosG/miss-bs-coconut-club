A day in Mission Beach can move easily from the sand to brunch, food or cocktails at Miss B's Coconut Club. The restaurant and bar is at **3704 Mission Blvd**, a short walk from the Pacific, with indoor seating and a large wraparound patio.

## Start With the Beach

Leave room for a walk along the beach or boardwalk and decide whether your group wants an early meal or a later stop. For breakfast favorites and mimosas, see the [daily brunch menu](/brunch). For a later meal, browse the [food and drink menu](/menu).

## Finish on the Patio

The [wraparound patio](/space) has fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables, weather permitting. Inside, there is a full bar, booths and tables. The current site lists weekday happy hour from 3pm to 5pm; call to confirm the day's offerings.

For directions and reservations, use the [contact page](/contact). Parking in Mission Beach can vary with the day and season, so allow time to find a space or consider walking from nearby accommodations.
