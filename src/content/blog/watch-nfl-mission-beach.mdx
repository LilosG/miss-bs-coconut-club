San Diego doesn't have an NFL team. That's just the reality. But Mission Beach has Miss B's Coconut Club, and on NFL Sundays, that's a better situation than it sounds.

Every game shows on the outdoor screens at 3704 Mission Blvd. The bar opens at 9:30am on Sundays — which covers every East Coast kickoff. Game day specials run all day, the kitchen is open until close, and the open-air coastal setting makes a six-hour Sunday of football feel like exactly where you're supposed to be.

## NFL Sundays Without a Home Team

When you don't have a local team, you pick a side and commit. San Diego's NFL fan base is a mix of transplants from every franchise in the league — Packers fans, Cowboys fans, Chiefs fans, Eagles fans — all looking for the same thing on Sunday: somewhere that takes the game seriously and is worth spending the afternoon.

Miss B's works because it's consistent. Every week, the screens are on, the specials are running, and the crowd shows up. You know what you're getting, which is worth more than it sounds when the alternative is figuring it out every Sunday.

## What Miss B's Offers on NFL Sundays

The full game day setup at Miss B's:

- **Outdoor screens** across the open-air bar and patio — games on from the first kickoff to the late window
- **Open at 9:30am Sundays** — East Coast noon games start at 9am PT, and Miss B's is ready
- **Game day drink specials** on beers, cocktails, and shots — rotating weekly, ask your server
- **[Brunch](/brunch) until 2pm** — full breakfast plates and morning cocktails through the early games
- **Full kitchen until close** — the [menu](/menu) runs all day, covering early games through Sunday Night Football

NFL Sunday windows run from 9am PT (early games) through roughly 7:30pm PT (Sunday Night Football). Miss B's covers the entire day.

## Green Bay Packers as the House Team

Miss B's has established the [Green Bay Packers](/blog/watch-green-bay-packers-mission-beach) as the house NFL team. Every Packers game — regular season through playoffs — shows on the outdoor screens with the full game day treatment.

Green Bay has one of the most transplant-friendly fan bases in the NFL. Packers fans end up everywhere, including San Diego, and they've found their home at Miss B's. For divisional matchups and late-season games with playoff stakes, the outdoor patio fills up the way a real fan bar should.

If you're a Packers fan, this is your spot. If you follow a different team, you're still welcome — multiple games show across the screens, and the atmosphere is the same regardless of which jersey you're wearing.

## Why a Coastal Bar Works for NFL

The default NFL bar is a dark interior with a lot of screens and not much else. That works. But it's not the only option, and for a full Sunday of football it's not necessarily the best one.

Miss B's is an open-air coastal bar in Mission Beach, steps from the Pacific. NFL Sunday with San Diego weather, a cold drink, and food that's actually good is a different proposition than the average sports bar. The outdoor patio gives you fresh air and space — important when you're in for four or more hours.

The energy works too. A crowd watching the Packers or any team they care about in an open-air setting has a different feel than the same crowd in a basement bar. Louder on the good plays, and easier to be in between them.

## What to Order

The [Miss B's menu](/menu) runs coastal-inspired food all day. For NFL Sundays:

- **Coconut Club Burger** — the go-to, no debate
- **Miss B's Favorites Tacos** — shareable, great for the early games
- **Poke Bowl** — lighter option if you're there for the long haul
- **Caribbean BBQ Chicken Salad** — works well for a full-afternoon crowd

For early games, [brunch](/brunch) runs until 2pm. The late East Coast kickoff at 9am PT lines up with brunch perfectly — eggs, mimosas, and football is a reasonable Sunday.

On drinks, Miss B's runs a full tropical cocktail program alongside draft beer and classic game day orders. Specials rotate weekly.
