Brunch in Mission Beach is as much about the setting as the plate. A useful brunch spot needs to work before the beach, after a morning walk on the boardwalk, or as the main event for a group that plans to stay awhile.

[Miss B's Coconut Club](/brunch) serves brunch **seven days a week until 2pm** at 3704 Mission Blvd. Weekday service begins at 10am; Saturday and Sunday service begins at 9am.

## What Is on the Brunch Menu?

The current brunch lineup is built around recognizable breakfast favorites rather than a separate weekend-only menu. Options include:

- Breakfast Burrito
- Breakfast Sandwich
- Breakfast Tacos
- Coco Açaí Bowl
- Easy B's Breakfast
- French Toast
- Pete's Biscuits and Gravy
- The B's Muffin

See the [full food and drink menu](/menu) for the current lineup.

## Mimosas and Morning Cocktails

Brunch at Miss B's includes mimosas and morning cocktails alongside the regular bar program. The open-air bar is one of the defining parts of the venue, with 24 copper flamingo tap handles behind the bar.

If your brunch group includes people who are not drinking, the value of the location is still the same: a full brunch menu, an indoor tropical dining room and an outdoor Mission Beach patio in one place.

## Indoor or Outdoor Brunch in Mission Beach

Miss B's has three distinct parts to the venue:

### Open-Air Bar

The teal open-air bar connects the interior with the patio and is available to walk-in guests during service.

### Tropical Dining Room

The indoor dining room offers booth seating, hanging plants and the original Miss B's mural. It is useful for groups that want shade or a more traditional seated meal.

### Outdoor Patio

The patio puts you directly into the Mission Beach setting with pink fringed umbrellas, fire pit seating and access to the bar. See the [venue and patio guide](/space) for more detail.

## Should You Make a Reservation?

Reservations are a good idea when a specific table time matters, especially for a larger group or a weekend visit. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar, but availability changes with demand.

Rather than relying on a fixed estimate for wait times, use the current reservation link on the [hours, directions and reservations page](/contact) if you need a confirmed time.

## Brunch for Birthdays and Group Weekends

Mission Beach brunch is a common starting point for birthdays, bachelorette weekends and other group days. If a normal table reservation is not enough, Miss B's also offers [private-event and group options](/private-events), including reserved areas and larger venue configurations based on availability and headcount.

## Build Brunch Into a Mission Beach Day

Miss B's is one block of a larger walkable neighborhood. From 3704 Mission Blvd you can combine brunch with the beach, boardwalk, Mission Bay or Belmont Park without needing to turn the day into a driving itinerary.

That is one of the reasons Mission Beach works well for brunch: the meal can be the start of the day rather than the only destination.
