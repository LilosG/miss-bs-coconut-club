A Mission Beach happy hour can be a quick drink or the start of an evening with food and friends. At Miss B's Coconut Club, the current listed window is **3pm to 5pm, Monday through Friday**. Weekend happy hour is not listed. Check current specials with the bar before making plans around a particular drink or price.

## Pick a Setting

Miss B's has a large wraparound patio with fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables. You can also sit inside at the full bar, booths or tables. The setting works for a drink, a meal or a group catching up after time at the beach. See [the space](/space) for photos and venue details.

## Look at the Menu

The [food and drink menu](/menu) covers Caribbean-inspired dishes, burgers, tacos, brunch, cocktails and beer. Ask which items are part of today's happy hour, since the selection can change. If you're coming with a larger group, [contact Miss B's](/contact) about seating or explore [private event options](/private-events).
