The best Mission Beach happy hour for you depends on what you want to do there. A drink after the beach, a seated meal and a gathering with friends call for different things. This guide offers a practical way to compare your options, then shows how [Miss B's Coconut Club](/blog/mission-beach-happy-hour-guide) fits those needs at 3704 Mission Blvd.

## Start With the Actual Schedule

Check the venue's current schedule before making happy hour the centerpiece of a visit. A weekday offer may not apply on Saturday or Sunday, and the items available can change. Miss B's currently lists **Monday through Friday, 3pm to 5pm** for happy hour; no weekend window is listed. Confirm the day's selection and prices with the bar. The [contact page](/contact) has the current posted venue hours and directions.

## Decide Whether You Want Food, Drinks or Both

A useful happy-hour stop lets everyone in your group find something they want. Look at the regular menu first, even when you're interested in specials: it tells you what kind of food and drinks the venue offers, while the staff can explain which items have a happy-hour offer that day. Miss B's [menu](/menu) includes Caribbean-inspired dishes, burgers, tacos, cocktails and beer. Kitchen service is available until **9pm**, which also matters if your group plans to stay for dinner.

## Match the Seating to Your Group

For an outdoor drink, check whether the venue has patio seats and whether those seats work for your group size. For a meal, indoor seating can be just as important. Miss B's has a large wraparound patio with fire pits, outdoor bar seating and tables, weather permitting. Inside are a full bar, booths and tables. See [the space](/space) for photos and ask about current availability.

## Think About Location and the Next Stop

Miss B's is on Mission Blvd in Mission Beach, a short walk from the beach. That makes it practical to build drinks or a meal into a day in the neighborhood. Use [directions](/contact) to plan your arrival. If your visit depends on a specific table or time, make a reservation instead of assuming patio space will be open.

## Plan Ahead for a Larger Gathering

A casual meeting and a hosted event need different arrangements. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar; [contact Miss B's](/contact) for a table reservation. For a celebration or a group that needs reserved space, review [private-event options](/private-events) and discuss the date, headcount and preferred area with the team.

The simplest comparison is schedule, current offers, menu, seating and group needs. For Miss B's specific happy-hour details, use the [dedicated guide](/blog/mission-beach-happy-hour-guide) and confirm anything that could change before you go.
