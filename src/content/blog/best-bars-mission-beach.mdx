Mission Beach has a compact bar scene concentrated along Mission Blvd and the boardwalk. That makes it easy to walk between places, but it also means the best choice depends less on distance and more on what you actually want: an open-air afternoon, a full meal, cocktails, brunch, or a place that can handle a larger group.

## What Makes a Good Mission Beach Bar

For a beach neighborhood, location matters, but it is not the only factor worth comparing.

- **Indoor-outdoor space:** Coastal weather is a major part of the Mission Beach experience. An open-air patio or bar gives you that atmosphere without requiring a table directly on the sand.
- **Food that can support a full visit:** If you plan to stay for more than one drink, having a real kitchen matters.
- **A full cocktail and beer program:** A good beach bar should work whether the table wants tropical cocktails, draft beer or a nonalcoholic option.
- **Daytime and evening flexibility:** Brunch, happy hour and evening service create very different experiences.
- **Group options:** Reserved sections and private-event space matter if you are planning a birthday, company gathering or weekend celebration.

## Miss B's Coconut Club on Mission Blvd

[Miss B's Coconut Club](/space) is located at **3704 Mission Blvd** in Mission Beach. The venue combines an open-air teal bar, a tropical dining room and an outdoor patio, so a visit does not depend on getting one specific type of seat.

The bar is known visually for its wall of 24 copper flamingo tap handles, while the drink program includes tropical cocktails, mimosas, beer and a full bar. If you want to eat, the [food and drink menu](/menu) includes Caribbean-inspired dishes, burgers, tacos, wings, seafood and shareable plates.

That combination is useful if your group is split between people who want a cocktail stop and people who want an actual meal.

## Daytime: Brunch and the Open-Air Patio

Miss B's serves [brunch in Mission Beach](/brunch) every day until 2pm. Weekday service begins at 10am and weekend service begins at 9am.

For daytime visitors, that makes it possible to start with breakfast or brunch and stay for the patio rather than choosing between a brunch restaurant and a bar later in the day.

## Weekday Happy Hour

Miss B's current happy hour runs **Monday through Friday from 3pm to 5pm**. Because hours and promotions can change, the [Miss B's FAQ](/faq) is the best place to confirm current details before making a trip specifically for happy hour.

## Going Out With a Group

For normal dining, use the reservation link on the [contact and visit page](/contact). Walk-ins are also welcome at the bar.

For larger groups, Miss B's has [private-event options](/private-events) that can include reserved areas, indoor dining configurations, outdoor patio space and full venue buyouts depending on headcount and availability.

## Where Mission Beach Fits Into a San Diego Bar Day

Mission Beach works well when you want a walkable beach neighborhood rather than a downtown bar crawl. The boardwalk, Pacific Ocean and Mission Bay are all close enough that drinks or dinner can be one stop in a larger day instead of the entire itinerary.

Pacific Beach is immediately north if you want a busier nightlife district after Mission Beach. If your priority is staying close to the beach and keeping the day casual, Mission Beach itself is easy to explore on foot.
